## Hi, I'm Meriç

**AI Automation Consultant | Computer Engineering Student**

I help small businesses and professionals find the work they still do by hand, then build the
automations and AI agents that take it over, and show the team how to use them. My tools are
n8n, APIs, LLMs and Python, and I care about one thing: a measurable drop in manual work.

I'm a 3rd-year Computer Engineering student, so I build in small, testable steps and publish
what I make. Everything on this site is my own work; demos are labelled as demos.

## How I work

1. **Find the painful task.** One manual job in operations, sales or finance, and how many hours
   it eats each week.
2. **Build the fix with real material.** Your last few outputs, your template, the place the
   numbers come from.
3. **Show before and after.** "This took X. Now it takes Y minutes."
4. **Hand it over.** Setup first, then training, then maintenance if you want it.

Automations I offer: lead collection and enrichment, outreach sequences, lead qualification, website
visitor assistants, hiring flows (CV screening, candidate outreach, onboarding).
