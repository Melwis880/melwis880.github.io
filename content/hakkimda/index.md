I'm an AI automation consultant. I look for the work a small business or a professional still
does by hand, build the automations and AI agents that take it over, and show the team how to use
them. I build with n8n, APIs, LLMs and Python, and I aim for a result you can measure: how long
the task took before, and how long it takes after.

I'm also a 3rd-year Computer Engineering student, so I work in small, testable steps and publish
what I build. My two public tools, [flx and n8n-organizer](/projects/), come with tests, CI and
written case studies; they are my own tools, not client work. Any demo on this site is labelled as
a demo. What I'm focused on this month is on my [now page](/now/).

## How I work

1. **Find the painful task.** One manual job in operations, sales or finance, and how many hours
   it eats each week.
2. **Build the fix with real material.** Your last few outputs, your template, the place the
   numbers come from.
3. **Show before and after.** "This took X. Now it takes Y minutes."
4. **Hand it over.** Setup first, then training, then maintenance if you want it.

Automations I offer: lead collection and enrichment, outreach sequences, lead qualification, website
visitor assistants, hiring flows (CV screening, candidate outreach, onboarding).

## Why this site exists

I write down what I build and what I learn on the way. There are three kinds of posts:

- **Field notes**: a specific problem I ran into and how I solved it.
- **Research notes**: a question I looked into properly, with sources and numbers.
- **Case studies**: one project, from the problem to the measured result.

Every post is mine: I choose what goes in, check every number and edit the final text. When AI
drafted or edited a post, the note at the end says so.
